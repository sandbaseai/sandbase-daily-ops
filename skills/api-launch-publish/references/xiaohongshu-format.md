# Xiaohongshu Article Screenshot Format

## Purpose

Use Xiaohongshu to turn a strong Chinese Compare or Top N article into a readable discovery carousel. The job is to earn the click with a useful conclusion, then prove it with article screenshots and a concrete decision rule.

This is not a smaller LinkedIn product announcement and not a generic launch poster.

## Source Selection

Choose exactly one source article:

- **Compare:** when the hook is a choice, trade-off, or misconception.
- **Top N:** when the hook is a market map, shortlist, or category trend.

Do not use the owned product landing article as the primary screenshot source. It is the conversion page, not the Xiaohongshu discovery asset.

## Required Deliverable

Create one Markdown file at:

```text
xiaohongshu/zh-CN/<article-screenshot-carousel-slug>.md
```

It must contain:

1. A cover hook of 12-18 Chinese characters, preferably two short lines.
2. A 4-8 page screenshot storyboard.
3. Source-article anchors for every page, such as a heading, paragraph, table, or diagram.
4. A 300-800 Chinese-character caption.
5. Three to six relevant tags.
6. One first comment that asks a concrete, relevant question.

## Screenshot Rules

- Screenshot the authored Chinese article, not an invented news page or a provider dashboard.
- One page, one point. Crop text so it remains legible on a phone.
- Page 1 is a hook cover with an article excerpt in the background, not a generic product render.
- Pages 2-5 establish the problem, evidence, and decision rule. Page 6 may be a CTA.
- Use exact article text only after final editorial review. Do not fabricate customer logos, metrics, or source quotations.
- Keep visible type large. Avoid more than 45 Chinese characters in a single screenshot focal area.
- Do not place external URLs in the cover or caption body. A comment keyword or profile CTA is enough.

## Cover Hooks

Good hooks name a real choice or surprise:

- `做 Agent，到底选哪种搜索？`
- `别把 Sandbox 当成一台机器`
- `API 上线后，为什么没人用？`
- `Agent 不是缺模型，是缺执行层`

Avoid vague labels:

- `Exa Search 上线 SandBase`
- `产品发布`
- `AI 行业观察`

## Caption Structure

1. Conclusion in the first sentence.
2. One plain-language explanation of the trade-off.
3. A short decision rule for builders or FDEs.
4. A question that attracts the intended audience.

## Tags and First Comment

Use three to six natural tags, not a keyword cloud. Start with a practical question in the first comment, for example:

```text
你做 Agent 时，最难选的是搜索、浏览器，还是 Sandbox？
```
