# Xiaohongshu Format

Create a Chinese-native, evidence-led carousel from an approved source.

## Pages

Use 4–8 pages:

1. specific hook or decision
2. context and who this is for
3. key evidence or mechanism
4. practical comparison, steps, or example
5. limitation or common mistake
6. conclusion and next action

Add or remove middle pages based on substance. Each page needs one clear job and readable text at phone size.

## Caption

- open with the practical conclusion
- summarize what the reader will learn
- add context not already visible in the carousel
- use 3–6 natural tags
- avoid long raw URLs in the body

## First comment

Ask one concrete question that invites real experience, not generic engagement.

## Reject

- literal Blog screenshots with unreadable text
- an overseas launch poster resized to portrait
- unsupported metrics or testimonials
- generic hooks such as “震惊” or “AI 时代来了”
- copied English sentence structure
