# Channel Structures

Use these as structural checks, not fill-in-the-blank copy.

## LinkedIn

1. Concrete change or observation
2. Why it matters to the intended builder
3. Two or three practical implications
4. Limitation or boundary when material
5. One CTA and canonical link

## X

1. Strongest verified point
2. One sentence of context
3. One CTA/link

Use a thread only when each post advances a sequence. Do not split one paragraph arbitrarily.

## Discord

1. What changed
2. Who can use it
3. How to try it
4. Known limitation or status
5. Feedback location

## Xiaohongshu

Follow `xiaohongshu-format.md`. The caption complements the carousel; it should not duplicate every page.
