# Social Publication Matrix

| Channel | Job | Lead | Typical shape | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Explain product progress and relevance | What changed and why now | 120–250 words, short paragraphs, optional bullets | Read, try, or discuss |
| X | Earn attention with one precise point | Strongest fact, decision, or result | One concise post by default; short thread only when necessary | Read or try |
| Discord | Update active builders | What shipped and how to use it | 80–160 words, practical bullets allowed | Try and give feedback |
| Xiaohongshu | Chinese discovery and practical education | Specific payoff, misconception, or choice | Hook cover plus 4–8 evidence-led pages; native caption | Comment or save |

Never paste the same opening, title, or CTA into every channel. Use the canonical source for facts, then rebuild the argument for the audience and interface.
