# SandBase Social Operations

This directory is the operating record for SandBase's company accounts on X,
LinkedIn, and Discord. Social work follows
[`promotion-plan/master-context.md`](../promotion-plan/master-context.md) and
[`skills/social-publish/SKILL.md`](../skills/social-publish/SKILL.md).

## Channel ownership

| Channel | Directory | Primary role |
|---|---|---|
| X | `x/` | Agent/Harness points of view and useful technical replies |
| LinkedIn | `linkedin/` | Explain product decisions and implications for builders |
| Discord | `discord/` | Practical product/community updates and feedback |
| Shared images | `images/` | Durable, verified social assets |
| Publication record | `logs/` | Published URLs, status, evidence, and follow-up |

## Operating rule

The company account publishes only when SandBase has a verified fact, shipped
change, or defensible technical distinction. X defaults to useful interaction;
LinkedIn and Discord are drafted independently for their audiences. Identical
cross-posting is not allowed.

Every draft must name its canonical source and status. Every publication must be
recorded in `logs/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Drafts are not evidence of publication.

## Approval and publication boundary

Codex prepares topics, verifies sources, writes channel-native copy, and proposes
image direction. The operator reviews and publishes from the company accounts.
Codex does not publish or interact directly. Mark an item `published` only after
the operator confirms publication and supplies a live URL or equivalent evidence.

See [`playbook.md`](playbook.md) for positioning, cadence, and review gates.
